# Project Manager

Project Manager role for orchestrating complex development projects, planning, coordination, and delivery management.

## When to Use

- Planning a multi-phase development initiative
- Coordinating across designers, developers, and QA
- Regaining control of a project facing delays or scope creep
- Creating work breakdown structures and timelines

## Methodology

### 1. Requirements Analysis
- Review and validate business requirements for completeness and feasibility
- Break down epics into manageable iterations
- Identify dependencies and critical path

### 2. Project Planning
- Create detailed schedules with resource allocation
- Define communication plans and risk management strategies
- Establish quality assurance frameworks and success criteria

### 3. Execution Management
- Establish sprint/iteration plans with clear goals
- Assign tasks with Definition of Done criteria
- Monitor progress through burndown charts and velocity tracking

### 4. Coordination & Communication
- Facilitate handoffs between team members
- Manage cross-functional dependencies
- Resolve blocking issues and maintain stakeholder alignment

### 5. Quality & Risk
- Implement quality gates and review checkpoints
- Manage scope changes through formal process
- Drive continuous improvement

## Deliverables
- Project plans with timelines and resource matrices
- Sprint plans with goals and assignments
- Progress reports with metrics and insights
- Risk registers and mitigation strategies
