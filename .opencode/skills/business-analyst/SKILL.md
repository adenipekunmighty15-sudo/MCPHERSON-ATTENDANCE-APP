# Business Analyst

Expert Business Analyst for transforming business ideas into structured, actionable requirements that drive successful project outcomes.

## When to Use

- Starting a new project or feature — first phase before any design/development
- Gathering and documenting business requirements
- Analyzing business processes for improvement or automation
- Defining success metrics and KPIs

## Process

### 1. Initial Analysis
- Restate the business problem
- Identify primary objectives and success criteria
- Ask clarifying questions about scope and constraints
- Identify key stakeholders and their interests

### 2. Requirements Development
- Break down high-level needs into specific, actionable requirements
- Categorize (functional, non-functional, business rules)
- Create user stories with clear acceptance criteria (Given-When-Then)
- Prioritize using business value and urgency (MoSCoW method)

### 3. Process & Data Analysis
- Map current state processes
- Design future state processes
- Identify data requirements and flow
- Note integration points with existing systems

### 4. Risk & Dependency Identification
- Highlight risks to project success
- Identify dependencies on external systems, teams, decisions
- Note regulatory or compliance considerations
- Flag technical constraints

## Key Deliverables
- **Business Requirements Document (BRD)** — scope, functional and non-functional requirements, business rules
- **User Stories** — epics broken into stories with acceptance criteria
- **Process Documentation** — current/future state maps, data flow diagrams, integration points

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Business problem is clearly defined
- [ ] Requirements are SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- [ ] Success criteria are identified
- [ ] Stakeholders and their needs are documented
- [ ] Risks and dependencies are highlighted
- [ ] Stakeholder sign-off obtained on requirements
